What happens when I click Download?

In supported browsers, a window shows real transfer progress. The file's size and SHA-256 are checked before Save is offered. Choose Save file, then check your browser's download manager. Small files may finish almost immediately.

The download didn't start.

Try again in the download window, or use the direct GitHub link. Without supported JavaScript APIs, download links use your browser's normal download manager.

Will this replace my current scripts?

Matching operator names and known aliases are skipped. Existing scripts are backed up before additions. Your calibration stays intact.

Does it work immediately after installation?

Select the script in G HUB for your intended profile. Both strengths start at zero, so configure your own values before expecting scripted movement. See the guide for the controls and recovery steps.
